Terms & Conditions

These policies apply to every trip booked with The Wanderlust.pk.

1Pickup & Departure Policy

  • Departure schedule: Normal departure is every Wednesday, early morning, from Islamabad.
  • Punctuality: Participants must arrive at the communicated pickup point prior to the reporting/departure time. Reaching on time is the sole responsibility of the participant.
  • Late arrival / missed pickup: Missing departure due to late arrival or failure to follow instructions will be treated as a cancellation, and no refund will be provided unless approved by the organizer.
  • Special pickups: Any special pickup arrangements must be pre-confirmed with the organizer before departure.

2Booking & Payment Policy

  • Advance payment: A 50% advance payment is compulsory to confirm a reservation. Bookings are confirmed only after payment is received.
  • Remaining balance: The remaining balance is due strictly at the time of departure.

KIDS POLICY & CHARGES

  • Under 3 years: Free of charge (without dedicated seat).
  • 3 to 8 years: 50% charge (provided with jumper seat).
  • 8 years & above: Considered adults and charged full package rate.

3Cancellation & Refund Policy

Seat cancellation deductions are strictly applied based on notice duration prior to departure:

Cancellation notice period Deduction rate
7 days before the trip 50% deduction
5 days before the trip 75% deduction
3 days before the trip 100% deduction (no refund)
  • Early departure: No refund is provided if a participant leaves the trip at any stage, regardless of the reason.
  • Trip cancellation by organizer: If the organizer cancels a trip, participants will receive a full 100% refund of the advance payment.

4Trip Plan Changes & Fuel Adjustments

  • Itinerary modifications: The itinerary may be changed, shortened, delayed, or rearranged due to weather, road blockages, political/security situations, or government restrictions in the interest of safety.
  • Extra expenses: Unforeseen costs caused by landslides or road blockages are not included in the package unless explicitly agreed.
  • Fuel price hike: In case of fuel price increases before or during the journey, additional costs will be charged accordingly.

5Ethical Conduct & Participant Behavior

  • Code of conduct: Respectful behavior toward fellow travelers, guides, drivers, hotel staff, and locals is mandatory. Misbehavior, abusive language, harassment, or violence is strictly prohibited.
  • Cultural respect: Respect local customs, religions, traditions, and community practices.
  • Prohibited items: Weapons, illegal items, and drugs are strictly banned. Violators will be removed immediately without warning or refund.

6Personal Belongings & Safety Responsibilities

  • Personal valuables: Participants are solely responsible for their belongings (cash, phones, luggage). The organizer holds no liability for theft, loss, or damage.
  • Safety & risks: Mountain travel carries inherent risks (terrain, weather, natural disasters). Participants must comply with safety guidelines issued by tour leaders and authorities.
  • Travel insurance: Trips are non-insured. Travelers are encouraged to secure personal travel and medical insurance independently.

7Environmental Policy & Detailed Terms

  • Members indulging in unethical activity, misbehavior, or drug use will be terminated from the tour immediately without refund.
  • No illegal or prohibited items, weapons, or drugs are permitted under any circumstances.
  • On mountain routes, vehicle AC will be turned on and off periodically to prevent engine overheating.
  • The company and organizers are not liable for natural calamities, avalanches, landslides, crevasse falls, or unforeseen accidents.
  • Respect all regional, cultural, and geographical affiliations. Non-compliance will be dealt with strictly.
  • Littering and environmental damage are strictly prohibited; our tours are strictly eco-friendly.
